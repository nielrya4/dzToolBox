# dzToolBox Method Citations

This document lists the methods implemented in dzToolBox and their corresponding citations in GSA (Geological Society of America) format.

---

## Univariate Visualization

### Probability Density Plots (PDP)

Probability density plots for visualizing detrital zircon age distributions.

**Citations:**
- Jessberger, E.K., Huneke, J.C., Podosek, F.A., and Wasserburg, G.J., 1980, High resolution argon analysis of neutron-irradiated Apollo 16 rocks and separated minerals: Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta, v. 38, p. 1001–1015.
- Hurford, A.J., Fitch, F.J., and Clarke, A., 1984, Resolution of the age structure of the detrital zircon populations of two Lower Cretaceous sandstones from the Weald of England by fission track dating: Geological Magazine, v. 121, p. 269–277.
- Dodson, M.H., Compston, W., Williams, I.S., and Wilson, J.F., 1988, A search for ancient detrital zircons in Zimbabwean sediments: Journal of the Geological Society, v. 145, p. 977–983.

### Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)

Kernel density estimation for visualizing detrital zircon age distributions.

**Citations:**
- Vermeesch, P., 2012, On the visualisation of detrital age distributions: Chemical Geology, v. 312–313, p. 190–194, doi:10.1016/j.chemgeo.2012.04.021.

### Histograms

Histogram visualization for detrital zircon age data.

**Note:** No specific citation required for histograms as they are a standard statistical visualization method.

## Maximum Depositional Age (MDA)

Methods for calculating maximum depositional ages from detrital zircon U-Pb data.

### General MDA References

- Dickinson, W.R., and Gehrels, G.E., 2009, Use of U-Pb ages of detrital zircons to infer maximum depositional ages of strata: A test against a Colorado Plateau Mesozoic database: Earth and Planetary Science Letters, v. 288, p. 115–125, doi:10.1016/j.epsl.2009.09.013.
- Coutts, D.S., Matthews, W.A., and Hubbard, S.M., 2019, Assessment of widely used methods to derive depositional ages from detrital zircon populations: Geoscience Frontiers, v. 10, p. 1421–1435, doi:10.1016/j.gsf.2018.11.002.
- Vermeesch, P., 2020, Maximum depositional age estimation revisited: Geoscience Frontiers, v. 12, p. 843–850, doi:10.1016/j.gsf.2020.08.008.

### Method-Specific Citations

| Method | Citation |
|--------|----------|

| YGF (Youngest Graphical Peak) | Saylor, J.E., Sundell, K.E., Perez, N.D., Hensley, J.B., McCain, P., Runyon, B., Alvarez, P., Cárdenas, J., Usnayo, W.P., and Valer, C.S., 2023, Basin formation, magmatism, and exhumation document southward migrating flat-slab subduction in the central Andes: Earth and Planetary Science Letters, v. 606, p. 118050. |
| MLA (Maximum Likelihood Age) | Vermeesch, P., 2021, Maximum depositional age estimation revisited: Geoscience Frontiers, v. 12, p. 843–850, doi:10.1016/j.gsf.2020.08.008. |

## Intersample Comparison

### Similarity

**Citations:**
- Bhattacharyya, A., 1943, On a measure of divergence between two statistical populations defined by their probability distributions: Bulletin of the Calcutta Mathematical Society, v. 35, p. 99–109.
- Gehrels, G.E., 2000, Introduction to detrital zircon studies of Paleozoic and Triassic strata in western Nevada and northern California, *in* Soreghan, M.J., and Gehrels, G.E., eds., Paleozoic and Triassic paleogeography and tectonics of western Nevada and northern California: Geological Society of America Special Paper 347, p. 1–17.

### Likeness (Mismatch)

**Citations:**
- Amidon, W.H., Burbank, D.W., and Gehrels, G.E., 2005, Construction of detrital mineral populations: Insights from mixing of U-Pb zircon ages in Himalayan rivers: Basin Research, v. 17, p. 463–485, doi:10.1111/j.1365-2117.2005.00279.x.
- Satkoski, A.M., Wilkinson, B.H., Hietpas, J., and Samson, S.D., 2013, Likeness among detrital zircon populations—An approach to the comparison of age frequency data in time and space: Geological Society of America Bulletin, v. 125, p. 1783–1799, doi:10.1130/B30888.1.

### Cross-Correlation (R²)

**Citations:**
- Saylor, J.E., Stockli, D.F., Horton, B.K., Nie, J., and Mora, A., 2012, Discriminating rapid exhumation from syndepositional volcanism using detrital zircon double dating: Implications for the tectonic history of the Eastern Cordillera, Colombia: Geological Society of America Bulletin, v. 124, p. 762–779, doi:10.1130/B30534.1.
- Saylor, J.E., Knowles, J.N., Horton, B.K., Nie, J., and Mora, A., 2013, Mixing of source populations recorded in detrital zircon U-Pb age spectra of modern river sands: The Journal of Geology, v. 121, p. 17–33, doi:10.1086/668683.
- Saylor, J.E., and Sundell, K.E., 2016, Quantifying comparison of large detrital geochronology data sets: Geosphere, v. 12, p. 203–220, doi:10.1130/GES01237.1.

### KS Test (D value)

**Citations:**
- Massey, F.J., Jr., 1951, The Kolmogorov-Smirnov test for goodness of fit: Journal of the American Statistical Association, v. 46, p. 68–78.
- Berry, R.F., Jenner, G.A., Meffre, S., and Tubrett, M.N., 2001, A North American provenance for Neoproterozoic to Cambrian sandstones in Tasmania?: Earth and Planetary Science Letters, v. 192, p. 207–222.
- DeGraaff-Surpless, K., Graham, S.A., Wooden, J.L., and McWilliams, M.O., 2002, Detrital zircon provenance analysis of the Great Valley Group, California: Evolution of an arc-forearc system: Geological Society of America Bulletin, v. 114, p. 1564–1580.

### Kuiper Test (V value)

**Citations:**
- Kuiper, N.H., 1960, Tests concerning random points on a circle: Proceedings of the Koninklijke Nederlandse Akademie van Wetenschappen, Series A, v. 63, p. 38–47.
- Press, W.H., Teukolsky, S.A., Vetterling, W.T., and Flannery, B.P., 2007, Numerical recipes: The art of scientific computing (3rd ed.): Cambridge, Cambridge University Press, 1235 p.

### Multidimensional Scaling (MDS)

**Citations:**
- Vermeesch, P., 2013, Multi-sample comparison of detrital age distributions: Chemical Geology, v. 341, p. 140–146, doi:10.1016/j.chemgeo.2013.01.010.

## Mixture Modeling

### Top-Down Unmixing

Inverse modeling to estimate source contributions to a mixed sediment sample.

**Citations:**
- Sundell, K.E., and Saylor, J.E., 2017, Unmixing detrital geochronology age distributions: Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, v. 18, p. 2872–2886, doi:10.1002/2016GC006774.

---

## Multivariate Analysis

### Tensor Factorization

Constrained Tucker-1 tensor decomposition for multivariate detrital geochronology, integrating kernel density estimation with matrix-tensor factorization.

**Citations:**
- Graham, N., Richardson, N., Friedlander, M.P., and Saylor, J., 2025, Tracing sedimentary origins in multivariate geochronology via constrained tensor factorization: Mathematical Geosciences, v. 57, p. 601–628, doi:10.1007/s11004-024-10175-0.

### 2D Heatmaps / Bivariate KDE

Two-dimensional kernel density estimation for quantitative comparison of bivariate distributions in detrital geochronology and geochemistry.

**Citations:**
- Sundell, K.E., and Saylor, J.E., 2021, Two-dimensional quantitative comparison of density distributions in detrital geochronology and geochemistry: Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, v. 22, e2020GC009559, doi:10.1029/2020GC009559.
